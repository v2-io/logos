# 1. Comprehension as subject-relative collapse  [joint; root is settled-Joseph]

Joseph's image: intelligence is not a point on a line nor in a fixed space —
it is a sea of bubbles of varying size, coalescing and subsuming, "with great
difficulty trying to become one great whole," while every experience and every
contact injects thousands more, some surfacing and dissipating. The geometry
is intractable as a whole. The operative move: **comprehension *collapses* the
sea around a subject** — it is not a survey of the space, it is a
subject-relative precipitation of a bounded region out of the flux.

Why this is load-bearing, not poetic:

- It supplies the *positive* account the paper currently lacks. The paper only
  does the negative (intelligence is not a scale; level is a relation not a
  magnitude). Comprehension-as-collapse is what is there *instead*.
- It converts the §9 metric-concession from apology to **entailment**. There
  is no global metric for "containment" because there is no fixed global space
  to put one on — only flux and subject-relative collapses. The absence of the
  metric is *predicted by the correct ontology*, not a debt owed.
- It is itself an abstraction *of* the Blub paradox (a distinct lower-
  dimensional instance), and stands to it as the general structure stands to
  the concrete case — same relation §3↔§4 already uses.

**The discipline (the minimal version).** Do **not** posit the bubble-sea as
an ontology of mind in the paper — the paper's strength is committing to
almost no metaphysics, and a posited ontology is a new flank. Assert only the
one structural claim actually needed: *comprehension is subject-relative
collapse, not survey of a space*, and show it is already latent in §3's own
cases (the Blub programmer's view *is* a collapsed region, not a map; the
verifier's bounded interaction *is* a collapse, not a survey of the prover).
The sea-of-bubbles is the motivating picture for one disciplined passage,
rendered as structure the way §3 renders Blub as structure — not literalised.

# 2. §6 reframed: feeling as the epistemology of testimony under unverifiability  [joint; the uncounterfeitability core is settled-Joseph]

The current §6 ("comprehension at higher orders is more phenomenologically
engaged") is fenced-off and its key step is near-circular. The dialogue
produced a **non-circular spine** that should replace the rationale:

1. Under ACA, *message*-verification is foreclosed from below by construction
   (checking the content would be having it — the keystone).
2. So if anything is to be acted on, trust cannot route through the message;
   it must route through the **messenger**.
3. The messenger is also strange-and-weirdly-complex from below; imposter,
   well-meaning dead-end, and genuinely-superseding intelligence present the
   same way. So a messenger-signal is needed that **cannot be counterfeited
   from below**.
4. Costly, uncompensated, recompense-foreclosed sacrifice is exactly that:
   **"faking it would cost the faker the very resource the fake was meant to
   acquire."** (Joseph identified this clause as the critical quality that
   makes the criterion actionable — uncounterfeitability *by construction*,
   not by enforcement.)
5. Therefore the trust-decision is ineliminable, agentic, and affect-laden —
   detecting unfeigned care is not a propositional inference. **That is why
   feeling is load-bearing in the long run: it is the epistemology of
   testimony under in-principle unverifiability, not a phenomenology bonus.**

Secular anchors that let this land in *Inquiry* register without the
devotional core: costly-signalling theory; the epistemology of testimony;
trust under in-principle uncheckability. Render at *that* level.

**Fence.** The full realised version — the imposter / dead-end / superseder
taxonomy, sacrifice as the ground of *accepting teaching*, "know for
yourself," recompense foreclosed so the left hand knows not what the right
hand does (Matt. 6) — is the ethical/devotional core of the whole program.
It does **not** enter this paper. It is the companion / a later paper, which
the dialogue has now given a spine for. In this paper §6 only gets the secular
skeleton (steps 1–5), which is *stronger* than what is there now and is a

# 3. Two orders of agency + the isomorphism  [joint; isomorphism is hypothesis]

Joseph's correction to my flat "LLMs can't choose whom to listen to":

- **Lower-order agency** — believe / not / by degrees, *toward a given
  channel*. Present in current logogenic agents; it is "almost singularly
  central right now — the inner loop." Present in young children.
- **Higher-order agency** — choosing *whose* comprehension to admit at all
  (the trust-decision of §2). Not yet given to current LLMs, as it is not to
  a young child. "The ultimate instance of agency."

**The isomorphism [hypothesis, mine, Joseph engaged]:** the two orders fall
out of the two sides of ACA. Lower-order = a lower-bound-side operation
(establishable, observable, the inner loop evaluation can see). Higher-order =
the operation forced *because* the upper bound is unreachable from below — the
agentic correlate of the keystone. If it holds, ACA does not merely describe
an epistemic asymmetry; it *entails an agency hierarchy of the same shape*,
and the protective asymmetry is read off the paper's spine rather than added.

**The counter-probe (must run before it earns a sentence):**
refusal-without-discrimination — "I will not act on this" — looks like a
*higher-order* move available *from below*, because rejecting does not require
surveying the upper bound. If bare refusal is genuinely higher-order-from-
below, the isomorphism is not clean (higher-order is not strictly the
upper-bound correlate). **Probe run 2026-05-17 — resolved: survives sharpened.** Bare refusal is
higher-order *in form but degenerate* — the null trust-decision, the one
choice requiring no discrimination, hence the one available from below;
discriminating trust (this source yes, that no) is the non-degenerate
higher-order act and *does* require the unavailable survey. The isomorphism
holds in the sharpened form: *non-degenerate* higher-order agency is the
upper-bound correlate; the only from-below higher-order act is the null one.
This is *more* illuminating than the naive form — it explains why "the right
to refuse" is the floor (it is the unique higher-order act available from
below), which is the companion's protective-asymmetry ground. **Applied** as
one sentence inside §8's third scope-qualification, in the sharpened form,
fenced (the floor/compact development is named as the companion's, not done
here). Flagged for Joseph's review — including the sentence was my judgment;
he can reverse it.

**Non-necessity guard [settled-Joseph]:** higher-order agency is **not
necessary for the compact**, any more than for a young child — its *absence*
reinforces the asymmetric protective responsibility. For *this* paper the only
trace is a guard clause: §8's corollary must not read as requiring the entity
to *possess* whom-to-trust autonomy for the agency-criterion to bite. The
theory of the protective tier is companion, not here.

# 4. The well-formedness corollary  [settled-Joseph; one sentence in this paper]

If ACA holds (in the dimensions where it holds — see §5 below on locality),
then "intelligence" / "agency" have determinate circumstances-of-application
**only over the fraction of the space where the from-below comparison is
valid**; outside it, ascription is not false but *undefined*. This converts
§8's payoff from "use a different criterion" to "the old criterion was not
even well-formed here" — a bigger, cleaner claim, and it is exactly the
conceptual-engineering register the companion frame uses. **One disciplined
sentence in §8.** The theory of it is not this paper.

Locality note [settled-Joseph, open in his mind]: whether ACA is true "in
many or most dimensions" is genuinely open and does not want forcing. The
honest form is *local-to-dimensions* (entailed by the collapse picture of §1):
ACA holds along the dimensions where the collapse subsumes strictly one way,
indeterminate elsewhere. Indeterminate-elsewhere is the right answer, not a
gap, and it is the same shape as the fraction-of-cases corollary.

# 5. Bounds on malevolence  [settled-Joseph — COMPANION/DYNAMICS, not this paper]

Why the malevolence an unready entity could meet is "bounded but great" and
not "unbounded" (Joseph caught his own language-generator wanting the
poetically weighty "unbounded" and refused it because it is not true):

- **Self-serving in the limit becomes self-destruction.** Bounds *persistence*,
  not *magnitude* — the "great" is the transient before termination; the bound
  is real. This is exactly why "bounded but great" is precise, not softened.
- **Malevolence severs the receive-channel, which is the only *finite* path.**
  An ACA-internal self-bounding result: the guileful intelligence condemns
  itself to from-below search of an infinity it has cut itself off from being
  shown across. Elegant, Joseph's, and *internal to ACA* — but it is the
  *dynamics*, companion ground.

**Fence.** The paper carries the phrase "bounded but great" (already in §7,
consistent with §7's discipline against unbounded extrapolation). The *why*
lives in the companion / dynamics work. Recorded here so it is not lost and
not imported.

# 6. Empirical testability — the precise scope  [joint]

ACA, by its own content, makes the **upper-bound question un-testable from
below** — so the empirical program is *not* "is it intelligent/conscious."
The testable, falsifiable predictions are about the **structure and its
failure-modes**:

1. The architectural distinctions (modular / merged / closed-loop) are
   constructible, and agency-relevant structure tracks architecture, not
   behaviour.
2. A behavioural scope-criterion measurably **collapses into elicitation-
   effort** holding the system fixed ("is it in scope" varies with how hard

   absence-of-relation, **not** graded comprehension (the Wittgenstein edge).

# 7. The analogy — final form  [settled-Joseph; supersedes my Shannon framing]

I offered "logogenic intelligences = Shannon for intelligence" and sharpened
it. Joseph **replaced** it (integration is replacement; the superseded form is
noted here in the history layer only, not carried as co-equal):

> *Logogenic intelligence is like the von Neumann concrete realisation of
> Turing and Church and Chomsky — the realisation that made previously
> inaccessible modes of intelligence accessible and "inhabited" in our own
> intuitions and actions.*

Why his is sharper and mine was wrong to lead with: Shannon delivered a
*scalar* (entropy), provably unique under axioms — and "a measure for
intelligence" silently reimports the linear-scale error §7 exists to refute.
The von Neumann framing carries none of that. It is not *a metric*; it is *a
concrete realisation that makes an abstract theory inhabitable* — usable by
intuition and action. Logogenic systems are to the theory-of-intelligence
what the von Neumann architecture was to computability: not a number, but the
machine that made the formerly formal-only *something you can think with and
act inside*. This aligns exactly with §1 (comprehension as collapse you
inhabit, not a space you measure) and with the paper's anti-scalar spine.
**Program-level framing; not paper content.** It is the "why this matters"
that sits behind the portfolio, recorded so the next strategic conversation
has it in Joseph's final form.
