# OUT.asymmetric-comprehension.md — the standalone asymmetric-comprehension paper (paper 3c)

*The **third** paper in this paper-dir. Not a rewrite of `granted-agency`: it
develops the asymmetric-comprehension argument (ACA) — deferred to "companion
work" in both prior stems — as a first-class contribution, with agency-extension
as the corollary (§8). Architecture, locked decisions, and the open naming
question are in [`ACA-DRAFT-GUIDE.md`](ACA-DRAFT-GUIDE.md). Title comes from
[`meta.asymmetric-comprehension.md`](meta.asymmetric-comprehension.md) via the
per-stem meta override (see `bin/build.README.md`). Build:
`bin/build 03-inquiry-ai-agents asymmetric-comprehension`.*

*Composed at natural length; no compression until the whole is in place
(project methodology). This manifest lists only segments that exist — `bin/build`
hard-fails on a missing segment, so rows are added as sections land. The full
ten-section plan-of-record is in `ACA-DRAFT-GUIDE.md`. Source-of-truth segments
in `src/c/`; paper-agnostic front/back reused from `src/`. `bin/build` reads
only the first five columns; the rest are advisory.*
