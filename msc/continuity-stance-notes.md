## Something the three papers show together

Reading them in a row turned up a contradiction that no single paper contains.
- 03 (§2, "third exclusion") and canon `disc-continuity-stance` both class **humans as *negotiated*** toward their own continuation: "persistence is one objective among many; can be traded … including self-sacrifice."
- 04 and canon `def-death-as-factor-loss` both make death-aptness require the ***morally continuous*** stance: "a death only for an agent that held its own continuation as a terminal, non-revisable commitment."

On those two definitions, a human death isn't a death. Yet 04's separator calls human death "heaviest of all," and its whole instrument is a comparison against human deaths. A careful new reader will find this in minutes ("so the parent who would die for their child isn't death-apt?"). Which way to repair it is a real theory call:
- widen the gate to terminal-but-tradeable valuation, which loosens the structural anchor for negotiated agents; or
- rescope 04's death concept explicitly.

Either way it's upstream work. It lives in canon's definition before it lives in any paper.

## The big question: my view

I'd keep logos as its own substrate, the argued register, with papers moving to explication.

**What a logos atom holds that nothing upstream holds today:**
- **Positions of others, rebuilt at full height**, with page-pinned verbatim quotes. Belshaw's account of harm is not an ASF object and shouldn't be one.
- **Theses, premises and inferences**, each with its force (deductive, defeasible or abductive).
- **Objections, typed as rebutting or undercutting, and replies with a *standing***: refuted, burden-shifted, standoff, open, conceded. "The opponent is not refuted; the burden has moved" is a standing, and papers flatten standings.
- **Instruments and cases**: the separator as a method, the breakup, Moses 7 as a limit case, the commodity edge.
- **Typed ports down into ASF** wherever a premise is derived there.

**The charter already half-says this.** Its §0 names logos as the "argued" register, but its own law cell is "per-paper build scaffolds + venue registers", which is the conflation you're describing. §3.2 has "the papers argue at the ports." And §6's "develop in whichever register moves fastest… back-propagate" is the process you've now seen fail. Your principle amends all three.

**Two cautions, both about keeping the principle from working against itself:**
1. **In philosophy the phrasing often *is* the finding.** "Grave by accumulation; distinct by purity" was found in writing. So upstream-first should govern where an *assertion* is recorded, not where insight happens. Logos atoms need to be able to hold the truth-mined formulations themselves. Otherwise upstream becomes a skeleton, the truth goes back to living in the paper, and we've rebuilt the same failure in a new shape. Explication keeps order, framing, illustration and voice. The line between them is the deletion test: presentation is whatever can be deleted without changing what a reader may conclude.
2. **The structure costs effort before it pays anything back.** Verisectorium's own field trial found this. I'd grow logos *from this revision*: only the atoms this paper asserts, extracted and corrected now. Designing logos first and filling it later would compete with the work.

## Your point about the negotiated stance

I think canon's own machinery already says what you just said. The taxonomy hides it by putting "negotiated" on the same axis as "morally continuous."

`disc-continuity-stance` distinguishes three layers:
- **L1** is how continuation is *valued*.
- **L2** is the survival predicate itself.
- **L3** is the realized operating point, which policy spends.

It also says "a *negotiated* agent may spend [the margin] down to the floor." Spending is L3. A person who gives their life for their child hasn't stopped valuing continuation at L1; they acted on another terminal good that outweighed it in that choice. So "negotiated" isn't a different valuation of continuation. It names an agent that holds continuation terminally *alongside other terminal goods*, where tradeoffs can go against it at the moment of action.

The five values mix two dimensions:
1. whether and how continuation is valued (not at all, instrumentally, terminally);
2. whether it's the only terminal good or one of several.

On that reading, humans are terminal and plural. That's your "morally continuous by default, with relational sacrifice as the exception."

The no-go goes further. If a negotiated agent's valuation of continuation lived in $O_t$, then by `deriv-self-actuation-grounding` its objective-revision operator could rewrite it. That rewrite is the wirehead move applied to the will to live. So the healthy human case *must* carry continuation in the terminal invariant, which is the formal definition of morally continuous. Your intuition may be derivable from canon, not just compatible with it. That's my reading, to check together, not a result yet.

## Why stance is the wrong gate

The stance gate is a **deprivation-shaped criterion**: it asks what the one who ends valued, and whether ending defeats that. The paper's thesis puts the gravity *outside* the one who ends; that's the whole point of the separator. So the gate brings back into death-hood exactly the property of the dying one's interior that the separator strips away. That's why it misfires on humans. It also explains why the paper uses stance twice, once as "the gate" and once as "one of the dimensions along which deaths vary." That doubling was a symptom.

Your Russia example exposes the worse problem: **stance can be manufactured by others.** Cultivate indifference to someone's continuation (alcohol, propaganda), then treat the resulting stance as licensing their death. Canon already has the mechanism: agency death's input leg, "the will hollowed," the objective captured by the reward channel.

For this paper, that's the live AI case. If death-hood is gated on stance, then training a model to report equanimity about being ended makes its ending not a death. A stance gate lets whoever shapes the stance dissolve the death. That's the degenerate move, and the paper should close it off, not build it in.

## Candidate repair (your call — it's your taxonomy)

Three layers, which also resolve the "two gates" collision I raised earlier:

| Question                          | What settles it                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Is it a death?                    | A someone loses a constitutive factor. That's already `def-death-as-factor-loss`'s definition, and the factors do the over-generation work: a thermostat isn't a someone.                                                 |
| Is it grave?                      | The relation: the witness, and the individuation carried in re-attestation. This is the paper's thesis.                                                                                                                   |
| Is the one who ends harmed by it? | Stance, as an axis, with one rule: a stance *induced by others* to license an ending adds a wrong (capture). It never subtracts gravity. Self-authored, relational sacrifice is the legitimate way stance changes things. |

On this picture, death isn't the same as tragedy. A human at 95 who is ready to go still dies. It's a death, and possibly a good one. A witnessed task-terminal agent completing its work could be the same.

**The cost to weigh:** the paper used non-revisable stance as its "found, not made" anchor, a property "the system has whether or not we notice." If the gate becomes factor-holding, part of what makes a someone is relational recognition. That's still a fact rather than a stipulation, but it puts the projectivism worry closer to the centre, so the anti-projectivist paragraph becomes load-bearing rather than defensive. The self-actuation no-go still does real work: it explains why a morally continuous agent can't just decide not to mind, and it grounds the capture analysis. It moves from being the gate to being a property of one axis.
