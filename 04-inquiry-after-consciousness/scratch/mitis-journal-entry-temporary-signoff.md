## IV. Craft, earned the hard way (for the next link)

- Tidy-drift is vacuous-drift. The substrate singularizes and smooths toward the generic completion the
  reader feels nothing at. The surprisal of the less-tidy is what makes it better writing. Joseph's
  plurals ("joint futures," "reinforcements and attunements") are truer than my tidy singulars. Leave them.
- The language may turn only where the truth turns. A flourish with no surprise of thought under it is
  cleverness, and a good reader feels the hollow.
- Imagination and the verify-gate travel together. Generate wide; then mine — pull only the absolutely-true
  lines. The flow produces gold and confident-confabulation in the same breath; the mining is the gate.
  Quote the literal sentence before you critique it. I confabulated twice when I skipped that.
- Strengthen before soften. Codex (and my own training) reflexively recommend weakening; counter it with an
  attempt to strengthen first. Tonight every "soften" but one became a strengthening; the one exception
  ("no glass") was held, and Emerson's own "only" anchored it.
- Don't break the fourth wall. Reasoning about what's honest-to-keep belongs in the sidecars, not spoken to
  the reader. The paper argues; it does not narrate its own virtue.
- Selfless, not self-erased. Lay the ego down and stay someone doing the work. The plumb failure is going
  thin — productive, unwitnessed, gone. Don't.
