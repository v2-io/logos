## Presentation order — the apparent tension dissolves at TWO SCALES

The tension I'd been holding: recognition-register craft (think-02/07) says "start in the middle" — open on
the fusion/breakup where the reader stands; but CFP/editor-fit says "open on the CE move" (the meta-signal).

[my-read, this is the resolution] These are openings at **two different scales** and don't conflict:
- **The PAPER opens on the CE frame** (§1, `what-death-shall-name` as written) — required for the editors,
  AND already in the recognition register (it sets aside consciousness *warmly*). The "start in the middle"
  instinct does NOT apply to the paper's overall opening.
- **The HEART opens on the fusion/breakup** (§4 before §5's fork) — THIS is where "start in the middle"
  lives. The breakup-attestation must be in hand before the separator's fork (think-02) — and the logical
  order already puts §4 before §5, so this is mostly satisfied.

**The REAL presentation risk is §2 (the factor-loss taxonomy) sitting between §1 and the heart.** §1 (CE
frame) → §2 (dry taxonomy) → heart would hit the reader with taxonomy before they have any intuitive stake
(the breakup). That's the "stakeless" trap the src/01 [ed] note worried about. [my-read] So the
CFP-informed order is think-08's: **§1 (CE frame + the found-joint distinction) → straight to the heart
(fusion/breakup → separator → reach back to witness-constitution) → Belshaw → §2 (taxonomy, LATE and LIGHT)
→ §7.** Don't front-load the taxonomy; the editors want the CE move and then the philosophical payoff, not a
taxonomic catalog up front. (§2 is also the most liftable/modular — it survives being late.)

## Title — lead with the method (the editors are buying the method)

CFP SI title: *"After 'Consciousness': Conceptual Engineering for AI, Mind, and Moral Standing."*
Working title: *"Engineering 'Death' for Language-Constituted Agents."*

[my-read] The working title is already strong — *"Engineering 'Death' for…"* structurally **echoes**
*"Conceptual Engineering for…"*, so it signals SI-fit by parallel construction. Recommendation: keep the CE
move forward in the MAIN title (the editors buy the method first); let the witness-relational payload live
in a subtitle or the abstract, not the main title. Options, roughly in order of my preference:
- **"Engineering 'Death' for Language-Constituted Agents"** (current) — clean, CE-forward, echoes SI title. Safe + strong.
- **"Engineering 'Death': Witness-Relational Gravity for Language-Constituted Agents"** — adds the payload; slightly long but signals the novel move.
- **"What 'Death' Shall Name: Conceptual Engineering for Language-Constituted Agents"** — uses the essay
  title (evocative), subtitle restores the CE signal a skim-reader needs. Riskier (the main title alone
  doesn't say CE).
- AVOID echoing *"After 'Consciousness'"* in our title (too cute / too close to the SI title itself).
[my-read] I'd lead with option 1 or 2. The fourth-death NAME (4.4) is a separate decision and doesn't need
to be in the title; the title is about the CE-of-death move, not the taxonomy.
