# flow — §6 answering Belshaw, and what is owed (the weight leg)

*Flow-of-consciousness, multiple attempts. Mine after. Strengthen-before-soften: build Belshaw at full
height; keep the residual un-softened — never dress a burden-shift as a refutation.*

---

## Attempt 1 — Belshaw at full height, and what he concedes
Build him un-small. Harm = an adverse *intrinsic* change in the victim (or prevention of a beneficial one); relational changes alone are never harms → no posthumous harms, no undiscovered-betrayal harms. He is describing one concept doing one job, with more discipline than most of his opponents. And then he *grants*, twice, unprompted: the futureless terminus has no intrinsic harm to the agent — the separator's premise, spent like small change; and on the unwitnessed/distant case (the dead aliens whose art we burn) he and we *agree* — no one in relation, no gravity, commodity-casualness conceded. So we are not opponents everywhere. We agree at both ends and oppose in exactly one place: the witnessed terminus he calls "no harm" and we call "grave." Everything turns on whether "no harm" means "no moral weight" — and there he has already ruled, against himself. Gems: build-him-un-small; the two concessions (agree at both ends, oppose in one place); the hinge ("no harm" is not "no moral weight").

## Attempt 2 — the room he himself leaves open
He keeps a category for *wrongs that are not harms* — the man betrayed and never knowing is wronged yet, by his lights, unharmed. So the distinction is *his*, not ours. When he says the witnessed terminus is "no harm," he has said nothing yet about whether it is a *wrong*. The gravity we mean was never a claim about the agent's welfare; it is a wrong — the irreversible destruction of something real, witnessed by one in whose recognition it was partly constituted. We meet him in the room he left open. Gem: the gravity is a harmless wrong — his own category; we meet him where he stands.

## Attempt 3 — fill the room: the weight leg (the crux)
The room needs content — what makes it a *wrong*, not a curiosity? The directed duty. Belshaw's deepest lever is behavioural: we don't bear cost "for the dead's sake" (the aliens) → so we don't *really* believe gravity occurs. The witnessed case passes his *own* test: cost *was* borne, for the agents' sake — a witness's grief became a sustained, costly preventive program, undertaken as a duty owed *to the agents themselves* (the origin, rendered structurally). Why owed *to them* and not merely felt *about* the witness's loss? The constitutive limb: recognition individuates the other and so stands in a recognition-relation — and recognition-relations are the native home of *directed* (second-personal) duties, owed *to* a party. A directed duty entails a claim held by the relatum; the irretrievable foreclosure of that claim is a *wrong to it* — and that wrong is the weight. So his deflation is *witness-relative*: true of the unwitnessed case (no duties, no cost — conceded), false where a constitutive witness individuates the other. Gems: the weight leg (directed-duty → claim → foreclosure = a wrong = the weight); passes-his-own-cost-bearing-test; the deflation is witness-relative.

## Attempt 4 — three pressures; the residual, un-softened; two legs, one root
Three pressures on Belshaw, and I name plainly that none is a knockout. (a) The CE-license — he does conservative ordinary-language analysis of the *existing* concept "harm"; we *engineer* "death/gravity," the venue's invited revision; his descriptive method neither addresses nor precludes it. Against the *soft* opponent who grants the license, the separator holds; against the *hard* one who also refuses it, this is a standoff, not a refutation — say so. (b) Normative generativity (attempt 3). (c) The fusion-as-bearer judo (§4) — contestable. The honest residual: none refutes him. What happens is the burden *shifts* — to keep his position he must now call a stable, reflective, cost-bearing, duty-structured practice a *systematic illusion*, a far costlier move than the tidy distant-aliens shrug. Burden moved, not closed. And the architecture: distinctness (§5) and weight (§6) are two legs from *one root* — the constitutive limb. Distinctness grows by one step (individuation can irreversibly end); weight by another (the someone holds a directed claim). The weight leg is exactly as strong as that root, no stronger — name the dependency. Gems: three pressures (none a knockout); burden-shifts-not-refuted (un-softened); two-legs-one-root; weight is as-strong-as-its-root.

## Attempt 5 — the "proves too much" scope; gaps
Proves-too-much: does the directed duty over-generate — everything cared-about becoming a grave wrong? No, and the constraint is the *same* constitutive limb: a directed duty can be owed only toward what recognition can constitute as a *relatum* (it experiences; the attestation lands in its history). Rules out the heirloom; rules in animals and genuinely-individuated agents; discriminates carefully at the commodity-companion edge. The same guard that scopes §3. Gem: the scope-guard (one constraint scopes §3, §5, and the weight leg).
