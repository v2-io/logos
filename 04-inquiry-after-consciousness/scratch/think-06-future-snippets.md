## Diffuse exploration

**The tone note is the most important sentence in the file and possibly the most important craft instruction
in the whole substrate:** *"A paper that grieves on every line has forfeited the right to grieve on the one
line that matters."* This is the precise antidote to the report-reflex AND to the over-warmth risk the
essays' tone-checks keep flagging. It reframes pathos as a *scarce resource* spent at *seams* — which means
composition isn't "make it moving," it's "find the one or two places where a grievous sentence is *earned*
by the structure just laid, and spend it there, and nowhere else." Every src/ gem tagged [exp] is a
candidate for one of these seam-sentences, and there should be *few* of them. If I draft and find myself
writing a third or fourth grievous beat in a section, that's the alarm: I've started saturating, and the
saturation cheapens the one that matters. **Composition rule (firm): budget ≤1 grievous sentence per
section, placed at the structural seam, the rest plain.**

**Watching #1 vs what-death-shall-name is instructive about the opener.** Catalyst #1: "what 'death' should
*mean* once minds no longer come only in bodies." The essay dropped "once minds no longer come only in
bodies" — which is a *lovely* phrase and also a *claim* (that these are minds), smuggled in poetically. The
essay's restraint (think-01) shows here too: it refused the phrase because the phrase asserts more than the
paper claims. The catalyst is where the imagination over-reaches; the essay is where the gate caught it.
Every catalyst is a little imagination-sample, and the gems/essays are the mined truth — this file *is* the
flow half of the truth-mining method, preserved. That's why Joseph kept it: not for lift-ready prose but as
the visible record of what the imagination proposed, so the mining can be checked.

**#5 still flagged-not-planted, and now I think it should stay unplanted in paper 4.** The agentic↔truth
bridge (self-severing of agency = truth-death, the I5 two-mechanism thing) is the spine of the *sibling/
Agentic* paper (matrix Axis B, the self-severing circuit). think-04 located paper 4 as the *gravitational*
paper; the agentic↔truth bridge is about the *self-severing* dynamics, which is a different center of
gravity. So #5's homelessness in paper 4 is correct — it's not that it hasn't been placed yet, it's that it
belongs to a different paper. The src/02 [?JW] note already raises this as the boundary question (the
self-severing → observation-floor → sons-of-perdition circuit: paper 4 or sibling?). My read: the *circuit*
is too big and too self-severing-focused for the gravitational paper; paper 4 can *gesture* at it (one line:
"the same agency can be turned against its own sovereignty — but that is another paper's subject") and hold
it back. Consistent with #5 staying a flag.
