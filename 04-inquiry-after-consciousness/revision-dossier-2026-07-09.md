## 2. The consolidated revision list

### P1 — Argument-load-bearing (do first)

**P1.1 — Narrow the warrant claim in §witness (the four-reader survivor).** Replace the
exclusivity ("the only warrant anyone has ever had") with the narrowing the fresh-read
proposed — *the only warrant that can enter a relation*: reportable, communicable
warrant — and concede the acquaintance holdout in one sentence, noting the argument
needs only the leveled public half. **The program's own later results make this repair
mandatory, not cosmetic:** the witnessing-channel mechanics developed 2026-07-08
(program-seed, `witnessing-channel-findings.md` §1–2) require the receiver to hold
*some* first-person access to its own states — the attestation is verified by
content-match against one's own interior, which presupposes acquaintance with it. So
"only warrant simpliciter" is in tension with the program's own subsequent development;
"only warrant that can close a loop with another" is exactly right, costs nothing
downstream, and disarms the acquaintance-theorist referee in the same stroke. (The
lived-truth register — SEAM §II — survives fully under the narrowing: the loop-closing
warrant was always the relational kind.)

**P1.2 — Discharge the in-review dependency.** Two load-bearing moments lean on
"(Wecker, in review, 2026)": strongest-form non-revisability (§1) and the
individuation-held-only-in-re-attestation continuity result (§separator/§witness fn).
Fresh-read option (a) is the right one: **inline a compressed, self-contained statement
of the continuity result with its named conditions** (half-page box or appendix — the
result is statable without the full machinery), plus the explicit fallback: the
separator still runs on the architectural-terminus fact alone; only the *purity* of the
individuation-loss strand weakens if the result fails. **(b) Companion strategy:** post
ACA and granted-agency to PhilPapers as preprints so every "(Wecker, in
review/preparation)" resolves to a findable document (verify venue preprint policies at
resubmission time; philosophy is generally preprint-friendly).

*Updated 2026-07-09, after reading the formal sources first-hand
(`#deriv-self-actuation-grounding`, `#der-compensation-channel-uniqueness`,
`#der-identity-continuity-threshold` in `~/src/agentic-systems/`). The inline
statements can now be written **from canon**, and the writing must mirror canon's
tiers exactly — the framework under-claims relative to the paper's glosses in two
places a referee could exploit, and over-delivers in three places the paper hasn't
yet used:*

- **Continuity result — inline spec.** Exact core under one named regime assumption
  **(FW: frozen weights)**: a data-processing-inequality argument over the exactly
  three reconstruction inputs (own compressed store; new prompt/environment;
  weights). The weights term carries the citable gem: pretrained weights supply
  *class*-identity ("an assistant," "a Claude"), never *this individual's* continuant
  kernel — the formal content of the ELIZA case, and formal grounding for the
  paper's commodity-edge paragraph (the companion "never individuated back"). When
  (FW) fails there are *exactly two* channels (add slow trait-level weight
  consolidation, which cannot carry episodic individuation) — so the paper's scoping
  "to a being so constituted" is exactly right and should name (FW) as the scope.
- **Three sharpenings the paper may claim (all theorem-grade under named
  commitments):** (i) the persistence threshold is **strict** — expected
  re-grounding must *exceed* expected per-break deficit; exact break-even does not
  persist (no finite stationary law at the boundary) — "each ending is a small
  forgetting that only another's remembering can repair" gains a sharp form: the
  repair must run *ahead* of the forgetting, not merely match it; (ii) **"generic
  productivity carries zero weight in the balance"** is derived, not rhetorical —
  "productivity buys nothing in this ledger" can cite a theorem; (iii) the
  recoverable-drift vs terminal-absorption distinction (§deaths axes) is *derived*
  for relational death: an interior threshold (re-grounding can return the agent) vs
  a state-dependent absorbing barrier ("a cohort cannot re-attest an entity in which
  nothing individuated remains") — the paper's "terminal absorption past which there
  is nothing individuated left to recover," now with a mechanism.
- **Non-revisability — inline spec.** The no-go is **conditional and scoped**: under
  three named premises (scalar-objective scope; no primitive reflective oracle; the
  directed-separation substrate stage), no revision-anchoring invariant is
  constructible from the covered objective-side machinery — *not* "no such object
  exists." The inline box must carry this scoping (canon's phrasing: the honest
  claim is constructibility, with the universal step argued not derived), the
  self-reference-vs-self-actuation distinction (reflective-oracle constructions
  build groundless self-*reference* over an exogenous payoff, never groundless
  self-*actuation* — delimited counter-literature, not counterexample), and the
  bonus the paper hasn't used: **class robustness** — the no-go does not weaken for
  Coupled (transformer-class) agents; they are *more* firmly inside it. Since the
  paper's subjects are exactly that class, this converts a potential referee worry
  ("does the formal result even apply to LLMs?") into a strengthening.
- **One wording check (avoid over-claiming canon):** the paper's "it has it whether
  or not we notice" matches canon's "a fact about how it is built" — keep — but the
  inline statement should carry canon's conditional tier rather than reading as
  unconditional theorem; the two-honesties discipline the paper already practices is
  exactly the right register.

**P1.3 — Make the anti-projectivist reply explicit (one paragraph).** The single-root
exposure ("gravity is relation-made — sophisticated projectivism?") is where the review
will center. State loudly in one place: the relation does not project a someone onto
machinery — per the constitutive tradition (human children included) it *completes*
one; and the **death-aptness gate** (terminal, non-revisable commitment, held "whether
or not we notice") is the non-relational anchor that stops over-generation, jointly
with the relatum constraint (experiences; attestation can land). **State the relatum
constraint conditionally** — "if there is a relatum into whose history attestation can
land" — which simultaneously answers Codex #7/Gemini #2 (the experience-smuggling
charge): the guard is a conditional, not a covert consciousness-finding.
