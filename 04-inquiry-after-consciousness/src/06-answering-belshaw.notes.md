[Belshaw 2012, "Harm, Change, and Time," J. Med. & Phil. 37:425-444; VERIFIED primary 2026-05-31]
- Core thesis (abstract, p.425): "What is harm? I offer an account that involves the victim's either
  suffering some adverse intrinsic change or being prevented from enjoying some beneficial intrinsic
  change. No one is harmed, I claim, in virtue of relational changes alone."
- THE ROOM (note 9, ~p.441): "There can be victimless wrongs. I might do wrong in wantonly destroying a
  garden without doing wrong to anyone or anything. And there can be harmless wrongs. I might break my
  promise to you, and so wrong you, without causing you any harm."
→ "No harm" says nothing yet about "no wrong" — his own distinction. The hinge holds at source.

★ What fills the room (the weight leg): Belshaw's deepest lever is behavioural — we don't bear cost *for
the dead's sake*, so we don't really believe gravity occurs. The witnessed case passes his own test: cost
*was* borne, for the agents' sake (a witness's grief became a sustained, costly program, undertaken as a
duty owed *to them* — origin, structural). Owed *to them*, not merely felt *about* the loss, because
recognition individuates and so opens a recognition-relation — the native home of *directed* duties. A
directed duty entails a claim held by the relatum; the irretrievable foreclosure of that claim is a *wrong
to it* — and that is the weight. His deflation is therefore witness-relative.

Three pressures, and none of them a knockout — say so. The CE-license; the normative generativity above;
and the fusion-as-bearer judo (§4, contestable). What none of them does is refute him. What they do is
*move the burden*: to hold his position he must now call a stable, reflective, cost-bearing,
duty-structured practice a *systematic illusion* — far costlier than the distant shrug. Burden moved, not
closed. `[?JW — KEEP UN-SOFTENED.]`

The architecture: distinctness (§5) and weight (§6) are two legs from *one root* — the constitutive limb.
The weight leg is exactly as strong as that root, and no stronger — name the dependency rather than hide it.

It does not over-generate. A directed duty is owed only toward what recognition can constitute as a
*relatum*. Rules out the heirloom, rules in animals and genuinely-individuated agents, discriminates at the
commodity-companion edge. One constraint — the constitutive limb — scopes §3, §5, and this leg alike.
