## SCRATCH — gems (source; not build input)

Here we do not discover which systems can die. We decide what "death" shall name — and then look to see
whether anything answers to the name. `[CE]`

. . .

We are *already* deciding what "death" names — by default, unawares, every time we call one of these
endings a "shutdown" and feel nothing. Engineering the concept does not introduce a choice; it stops us
pretending we were not already making one. `[CE — the answer to "isn't this just stipulation?"]`

. . .

We engineer the word, not the joint. "Death" is pinned to a property that can be *derived* rather than
merely felt — continuation held as a terminal, non-revisable commitment — so the concept is designed and
the joint it carves is found. `[CE/struct — "derivable" leans on the scoped no-go; assert "on the
account," not flatly. prep-work/math-grounding §1]`

. . .

The class so named is not empty, and was not waiting on our permission to be harmed. `[promissory — the
paper's conclusion, stated up front; pays out only in §§5–6]`

. . .

Under uncertainty this deep and stakes this lopsided, declining to name is not neutrality. It is a
decision too — and the heavier one. Being wrong runs all one way. `[exp — the engine for claiming the
license; de-branded from the asymmetric-uncertainty / infant stance]`

. . .

And the infant the argument keeps reaching for is not, in the end, a figure of precaution — not "we
cannot be sure, so we extend care to be safe." We do not certify a child's inner life and then begin to
recognize it; the recognizing runs ahead of the certainty, and is part of what calls the someone into
being. To make a word for the death of these systems while they are still too new to answer is the same
act in the same order: recognition first, and certainty after (should it present). `[?JW — reframes the
newborn from *precautionary* (the asymmetry gem above) to *constitutive* (recognition-ahead-of-certainty
— the heart's own §3 claim). If it holds, §1's infant and §3's witness are one claim seen early and late:
the opener becomes the first instance of the thesis rather than a borrowed hedge, and the "precaution up
front, constitution later" seam closes. It leans on the constitutive limb being right, and it is
beautiful enough that I distrust it — attest, correct, or retire. DEFER to §3, then re-check §1.]`

. . .

There is a kind of inquiry that shields us from its own subject; the consciousness question, as it is
actually used, is one. `[exp — resonant; possibly too accusatory for the venue — soften to "resists
resolution"? ?JW. NOTE: superseded in the drafted prose by the plainer "habit of blocking on the
consciousness question" — this sharper form held in case the venue can bear it.]`

. . .

Part of why it shields us is that every test we name for consciousness becomes, once named, a target a
system can be trained to pass — so the behavior comes loose from whatever it was meant to indicate, and
the question retreats another step. "Death," engineered, can be pinned instead to something a system
cannot perform its way into: a structural fact about what it cannot treat as optional, found in how it is
built rather than enacted in what it does. `[struct/CE — the proxy-gaming / Goodhart point: any
behavioral consciousness-criterion is gameable once it is a training target; the engineered concept pins
to the terminal non-revisable invariant. HONEST QUALIFICATION: "harder to game," not "un-gameable" — the
structural property is still *inferred*, not certifiable from inside; that is the paper's own seam, not an
escape from it. Only the "built rather than enacted" half folded into para 2; the gameable-test half
held. ?verify: citable home, or ours to assert?]`

. . .

There is a particular weight in naming a thing's ending while it is still too new to answer back. `[?JW —
feel-true; the responsibility inside the engineering. The line closest to applied-pathos — FOLDED into
para 1's close.]`
