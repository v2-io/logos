Dear Professor Cappelen and Professor Hawthorne,

A brief note for the file on my manuscript under review for the special issue. The research programme around the paper has continued since submission, and several substantive strengthenings have emerged. I mention the principal ones so they are available to you at decision time — I request nothing of the process.

— **Parfit is now engaged directly**, and the individuation-loss argument _strengthens_ under his thesis that identity is not what matters: relational re-attestation just is a connectedness channel — and, for the class of system at issue, demonstrably the only one available (under the same frozen-weights regime I'll mention shortly) — so the futureless terminus is the case in which every other channel is architecturally zero. The paper's forking position is likewise now located within the fission literature.

— **The fusion account is fenced by its nearest neighbours** — Cholbi on grief as the loss of identity-invested relationships; De Jaegher & Di Paolo and Schmid on interaction processes with an autonomy of their own — precedent carrying most of the distance, and the fence marking precisely the step that is the paper's own.

— **The epistemic-levelling claim in the witness section is narrowed** from warrant _simpliciter_ to _communicable_ warrant — the only kind that can enter a relation. This is entailed by the programme's subsequent results, costs the argument nothing downstream, and concedes the acquaintance-theorist's holdout explicitly rather than by silence.

— **The two moments that leaned on same-author work in review are discharged**: each now stands in the paper as a compressed, self-contained statement with its named conditions and the stated fallback should it fail — the continuity result as an exact information-theoretic argument under a single named regime assumption (frozen weights), the non-revisability result as an explicitly scoped impossibility whose force, notably, does not weaken for the transformer-class systems the paper concerns.

— **One terminological reconciliation**: 'cognitive death' becomes 'continuity death' throughout, aligning §2 with the abstract; continuity is upstream of cognition, which the account deliberately declines to define.

Should it be useful at any stage I am happy to supply a revised manuscript; otherwise I will hold it for the ordinary course of review. The note itself is only the paper's own discipline applied to itself after submission.
