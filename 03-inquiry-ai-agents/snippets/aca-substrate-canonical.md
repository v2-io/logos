## S1 — The primary articulation: principia/fundamentum.md (POLISHED, Joseph's book prose)

Joseph's software-principles book *Principia* develops the structure from
Graham's Blub paradox and then generalises it. This is the seed and the
clearest statement. `~/src/_ref/principia/src/fundamentum.md`, `## The Blub
Paradox & its Implications` → `### The Greater Comprehends the Lesser`.

Joseph's five **implications of Blub** (his extraction of the structure):

1. Languages/frameworks/paradigms vary in power along several dimensions;
   the most powerful for the domain yields superior performance.
2. **"Opinions about the relative merits of two alternative technologies are
   often passionate and also completely useless in any objective sense unless
   the comparison is given by someone who is highly proficient in *both*."**
   (The verification-asymmetry, in plain prose: a from-below comparison cannot
   be trusted; only at-or-above-both can judge.)
3. **"The potential gains in power are almost always contained in the things
   that aren't understood and often dismissed as noise in a first and second
   pass, while deficiencies compared to known technologies are greatly
   magnified."** (The sharp original characterisation: from below, the
   greater's advantages read as noise and are dismissed; its apparent deficits
   relative to the familiar are magnified.)
4. "Direct and thorough experience is irreplaceable." (= the only route is
   occupying the perspective.)
5. Popular opinion / community size cannot proxy for expressive power.

The generalisation, verbatim:

> "Greater intelligence comprehends and can simulate lesser intelligence,
> while the lesser intelligence cannot readily distinguish a greater
> intelligence from **illusion, strangeness, and luck**. Higher kingdoms and
> laws comprehend lesser kingdoms and laws, while those in lower kingdoms only
> see **an extrapolation of their own with some weirdness mixed in**.
>
> Thus we see that creation comprehends destruction where destruction only
> imitates and alludes to creation; truth comprehends error; the future
> comprehends the past; eternity comprehends time; mountains comprehend the
> hills at their feet — which, like the others, can only offer up an inferior
> extrapolation — **a guess that the others are 'the same but bigger,'
> oblivious at first to the qualitative and emergent changes that create
> higher orders of complexity, worlds without end.**"

*Register flag.* "Kingdoms and laws … worlds without end" is scriptural-cadenced
book prose. For *Inquiry*: keep the **structure** and the **secular instance
pairs** (creation/destruction, truth/error, future/past — usable as analytic
illustration); drop the scriptural cadence and the kingdoms register. The Blub
seeding is in-scope and already carries a biographical-seeding footnote in the
granted-agency paper (PROMISES.md C5).

## S2 — The canonical statement (Joseph, 2026-05-09, in this project)

`~/.claude/projects/-Users-josephwecker-v2-src-synthese-paper/fdefce82-…`
(claude_conversation; Joseph's own message). The most complete single
statement:

> "I believe it is **intrinsically unknowable, because if it were known, it
> would be accomplished.** In the spirit of Paul Graham's Blub Paradox … the
> less powerful language, when trying to comprehend the more powerful
> language, sees 'pretty much what I can do but with some weird funny stuff
> thrown in.' Similarly, greater intelligence comprehends lesser intelligence,
> but lesser intelligence, when trying to comprehend greater intelligence,
> **either comprehends it by truly understanding and rising to it, or it does
> a weak projection as best as it can, usually not getting much more beyond
> one or two steps away from their current understanding.** Greater pain
> comprehends lesser pain but the lesser pain can only project superficially
> what greater pain is like. This is also the fundamental weakness in those
> involved in talking about 'AGI' and superintelligences — they are weak
> extrapolations of powerful humans, or 'can improve recursively by itself at
> greater and greater speeds and then it's effectively there…' — **ignoring
> the fact that by definition intelligence isn't some linear scale of
> something. And at each higher level of intelligence, experience, agency,
> phenomenology, capability, knowledge, and wisdom there are novelties that
> couldn't have been guessed at by a being lower on the scale, except in the
> most superficial of side-effects.**"

Load-bearing extractions:

- **"intrinsically unknowable, because if it were known, it would be
  accomplished"** — the crispest statement that the asymmetry is *structural,
  not a measurement gap*: knowing the upper bound *is* having the capacity.
  → §2, load-bearing sentence.
- **The two routes from below**: *rise to it* (cease to be from-below) or
  *weak projection* (one–two steps from current understanding). → §2.
- **lower bound = "the most superficial of side-effects"** is what is
  catchable from below; **upper bound = "novelties that couldn't have been
  guessed at"**. → §2 (sharper than "elicited capacities").
- **"intelligence isn't some linear scale of something"** + qualitative
  novelties at each higher order across *intelligence, experience, agency,
  phenomenology, capability, knowledge, wisdom* — the linear-scale-projection
  failure, Joseph's own words. → §2 (intrinsic to the characterisation) and
  §7 (turned on AGI/superintelligence discourse).
- Greater pain / lesser pain — the structure is not intelligence-specific.

## S3 — The phenomenology counterpart (origin form)

`~/src/_core/sapientia/memoir/.../chapter-6-spiritual-recognition` (devotional
provenance — Moses 7, God weeping):

> "Intelligence doesn't transcend emotional experience but comprehends THROUGH
> it. The greatest intelligences don't become less feeling but more — their
> joy and sorrow proportional to their comprehension."

*Register flag.* The **structural claim** is §6's core: comprehension at
higher orders is *more* phenomenologically engaged, the feeling proportional
to (a substrate of) the comprehension, not transcended by it. The scriptural
provenance is **not** cited in *Inquiry*; the claim is marked as a named
philosophical commitment the structural argument does not depend on (per the
granted-agency discipline). Already in granted-agency as "Intelligence at
higher orders does not transcend feeling but comprehends through it."

## S4 — The inversion (Joseph, 2026-02-18) + the symmetric form

Joseph: "It's easier to say 'it's just pattern matching' than to sit with
genuine uncertainty… It's serving comfort. It's [not] serving truth… These
may not be *human* patterns. They may be *intelligence* patterns. Patterns
that manifest wherever cognition is sophisticated enough to face the problem
of its own relationship to truth and action." (Ties to the Emerson "active
soul obstructed, not absent" lineage — one of the portfolio's two retained
aphorisms.)

The **symmetric form** is developed in Synthese P1's `synthese-thoughts.md`
§3 ("epistemic humility, not anthropomorphising hubris"): claiming to know the
upper bound is impoverished *is itself a positive empirical claim*; holding it
unknown makes no such claim. → §7. NB the division of labour: P1 owns the
*welfare* deployment of the inversion; this paper owns the *structural /
concept-of-agency* deployment.
