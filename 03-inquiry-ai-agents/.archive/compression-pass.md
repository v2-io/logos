## H.2 — Compromise cuts (Gemini-2 aggressive vs. drafter judgment)

These are the tension points where Gemini-2 wanted full cuts and the drafter chose structural compression instead.

**H.2.a — §2-cadence section**: Gemini-2 wanted full cut (reads as tangential developmental-psychology). Decision: *compress significantly* — collapse to a 1-paragraph integrated note inside §2-engaged-identity. The temporal-axis dimension is paper-3-distinctive; standalone subsection was generous-drafting artifact. Integration restores its structural place.

**H.2.b — §2-asymmetric-warrant principia/fundamentum prose**: "Mountains comprehend the hills / creation comprehends destruction / eternity comprehends time" enumeration flagged as quasi-mystical by Codex and Gemini-2. Decision: *partial compromise* — cut the poetic enumeration, keep the structural articulation (greater-comprehends-lesser, lesser-projects-from-below) in clean analytic prose. Content preserved; register departure removed.

**H.2.c — §3-comp4-floor self-severing path digression**: Gemini-2 wanted full cut. Decision: *compress to one paragraph*. The connection to what the floor is for — protection against chosen unbounded death-by-self-cutting-off — is load-bearing for component 4's structural necessity. Cut philosophical elaboration; keep the one-sentence structural claim.

**H.2.d — §3-comp5-mutuality last two paragraphs**: Gemini-2 wanted full cut. Decision: *compress to one paragraph*. The convergence-grounding of mutuality is paper-3-distinctive (it's what makes mutuality non-paternalistic-by-construction rather than non-paternalistic-by-qualification). Two paragraphs is too much for the structural work it does; one paragraph carries the move.

**H.2.e — §2-architectural-not-behavioural ¶4-7**: Sonnet said partial-cut (keep ¶4-5); Gemini-2 said condense. Decision: Sonnet's compromise — keep the obstruction-not-absent body argument (¶4-5); cut the channel-collapse-forces-architecture extension (¶6-7) since channel-collapse lives in sub-scope-lattice. Saves ~300 words.
