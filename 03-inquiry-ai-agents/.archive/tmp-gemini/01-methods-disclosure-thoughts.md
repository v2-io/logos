Thoughts on 01-methods-disclosure.md:

1.  **Purpose**: Serves as the required AI disclosure for the journal.
2.  **Meta-Argument**: The author cleverly uses the disclosure itself as an example of the paper's thesis (the "granted-agency compact").
3.  **Critique**: While philosophically cute and recursively interesting, journals often have strict structural requirements for where these disclosures must live (e.g., as a separate declarative section at the end). 4.  **Trimming**: If space is tight, the justification for *why* it is in the introduction ("The disclosure is placed within the methodological preamble rather than appended...") could be cut, or the whole section could be moved to the back-matter where it traditionally belongs, referencing back to the paper's thesis if necessary. It's a non-load-bearing structural flourish.
