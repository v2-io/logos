Thoughts on 02-asymmetric-warrant.md:

1.  **Core Argument**: The section attempts to justify *why* the architectural/identity conditions are the correct ones, relying on an "asymmetric-comprehension" argument.
2.  **Critique - Metaphor Overload**: The core philosophical point (that our evaluation methods only establish lower bounds, and that lesser intelligences cannot fully comprehend greater ones) is almost entirely lost in a barrage of quasi-mystical metaphors: "Higher kingdoms and laws comprehend lesser kingdoms... Creation comprehends destruction... truth comprehends error... eternity comprehends time; mountains comprehend the hills at their feet". This is highly inappropriate for an analytic philosophy journal like *Inquiry*. It sounds like theology or romantic literature, not structural argument.
3.  **Self-Citation/Deferred Argument**: The section repeatedly states that the actual "deeper structural ground" is in a companion paper ([Author, in preparation]). If the warrant for the main conditions of *this* paper exists entirely in an unpublished companion piece, that's a major structural flaw. The argument must stand on its own here.
4.  **Trimming/Revision**: This section needs a complete rewrite or near-total deletion. The only salvageable point is the first paragraph's claim: we track architecture because behavioral evaluation is biased toward lower bounds. Make that point clearly and concisely (perhaps integrate it back into `02-architectural-not-behavioural.md`), and delete all the metaphor-heavy "asymmetric comprehension" text and the paragraph on infinite intelligence and feeling. This will save significant space and vastly improve the paper's academic tone.
