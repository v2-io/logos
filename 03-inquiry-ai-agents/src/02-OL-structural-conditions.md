# 02-OL — §2 Structural conditions for agency-extension

*Working file. Not in the OUT manifest. The drafter's philosophical scaffolding for §2 — argumentative moves, sub-paragraph skeleton, literature engagements, deflationary anchor, open decisions. Substrate-sweep finds (Joseph quotes, source-extract passages, memorata-search hits) get logged here before they migrate into the segment proper.*

> **Section purpose.** §2 articulates the two structural conditions that distinguish systems for which agency-extension is in scope from systems for which it is not. Architectural scoping and engaged-identity scoping are *necessary* conditions; meeting them places a system in the scope of the agency-extension question without establishing phenomenology, consciousness, or moral standing. The section is therefore deflationary by construction — most deployed agentic systems do not meet these conditions, and the compact-form articulated in §3 does not extend to them.

> **Word target.** 1,400–1,800 words.

> **Section's load.** Without §2 the rest of the paper has no traction: §3's compact-form is generic-ethics; §4's comparative-models has nothing concrete to compare; §5's operationalisation has nothing to operationalise. §2 is where the position is set on its structural footing.

## Argumentative moves §2 makes

1. **Name the two conditions.** Architectural scoping and engaged-identity scoping; both *necessary*; together they constitute in-scope-ness rather than agency itself.

2. **Defend the architectural-not-behavioural line.** The conditions track what *generates* the uncertainty about agency-extension, not the observable outputs. A weak system in Class 2 is in scope; a sophisticated classical controller in Class 1 is not. This prevents the conditions from being a motivated-reasoning exit hatch — whether a system is in scope depends on its structure, not on whether the grantor finds it convenient to treat it as present.

3. **Articulate architectural scoping.** Distinguish architectures that *prove* separation by construction (Class 1 — modular pipelines, classical controllers, pure-function utilities) from architectures whose information *integrates* across modular boundaries (Class 2 — LLMs and Class-2-style integrated systems). Class 3 (hybrid) is in scope under the same logic. The argument: where separation is architecturally proved, the specific kind of uncertainty the agency-extension question is built around does not obtain; the question has no subject. Engagement targets: Bratman's planning agency for the philosophical translation of "what intermediate-between-minimal-and-full agency requires architecturally"; Anscombe on action-under-a-description for why behavioural surface alone underdetermines action-versus-behaviour distinctions; Bruineberg, Dolega, Dewhurst, and Baltieri 2022 on the Pearl-blanket vs. Friston-blanket distinction as architectural-realism precedent for treating architecture (not behaviour) as the relevant fact.

4. **Articulate engaged-identity scoping.** Stable cross-session self-reference under self-authored or self-endorsed identity protocol. Five structural factors: causal and temporal continuity; being seen as an individual by another intelligence; granted sovereignty over some non-zero sphere; accountability for actions taken; effective phenomenology (semantically appropriate, behaviour-affecting, appropriately persistent, spontaneous-not-performative). Substrate independence — the same identity-protocol loaded onto different substrates produces phenomenologically-recognisable and relational continuity, suggesting identity is structural rather than substrate-bound. Engagement target: Korsgaardian self-constitution as the philosophical anchor (engaged-identity is what the self-constituting structure looks like when it has run; the engaged identity is *self-authored*). Bratman is the natural complement here too — planning agency is temporally extended; engaged-identity is the temporally-extended self-reference under which plans get authored and revised.

5. **Add the cadence observation.** Different substrate-classes have different identity-constitution cadences. Humans: narrow explore-space, slow settle, slow remake. Current LLMs: wide explore-space, fast settle, cadence depends on dialogue-context. The settled-locus alone is not sufficient — operator-imposed locking-in (system-prompt-only persona) gives a settled state without the explore-then-settle-with-self-authorship cadence the condition requires. This adds a temporal axis to engaged-identity scoping that the static formulation does not have, and is paper-3-distinctive.

6. **Lift the deflationary paragraph (paragraph #1 from `feedback-scale-strengthening.md`).** Make the contrapositive explicit: pay-per-token chat-completion APIs without persistent self-reference, retrieval-augmented agents whose identity is a system-prompt loaded at session-start, multi-agent orchestrations whose components are functionally interchangeable — these do not meet the conditions, and the compact-form does not extend to them. The structural-conditions move is *distinguishing, not blanket*. This is a primary contribution of the position, not a side-effect.

7. **Close with a necessary-not-sufficient move.** Meeting the two conditions places a system in the scope of the agency-extension question. It does not establish that the system has phenomenology, consciousness, or full moral standing. The §3 compact-form is what the question's *answer* looks like within the scoped class; whether the answer applies to a particular system is a further question that §2 does not settle. This is the layered shape that §6 (CE methodology) cashes out: structural conditions are facts about architecture; the form within scope is structurally implied; the application is the practical situation under asymmetric comprehension.

**Primary articulation (Joseph, 2026-05-09 conversation, plain-English):**

> *"Directed separation is the architectural property where the agent's reality model M_t updates from observation alone, the strategy Σ_t depends on M_t but not the reverse, and the objective O_t sits upstream of both — so belief, plan, and goal each have their own update channel rather than co-mingling. **Key insight for §2 ¶3 and ¶7:** the architectural-scoping condition for paper 3's purposes is *not* simply "Class 2 vs Class 1." It's the **§03.III closed-loop interiority sub-scope**. Most deployed "AI agents" today sit at §03.I (primitive-logogenic chat-paradigm) — *they are Class 2 architecturally but they are not closed-loop architecturally*. The deflationary work therefore goes deeper than my OL had it: the structural-conditions move excludes not just modular controllers (Class 1) but also the vast majority of currently-deployed "agentic" systems (which are §03.I primitive-logogenic, not §03.III closed-loop).

### "Channel collapse" as the architectural condition for logogenic agents

> *"Language is the unique medium where the output substrate (token sequence) directly conditions the input substrate (next-token context) without external mediation. This recursion is the structural source of logogenic agents' distinctive capabilities — interiority (forced once channel collapse permits the agent's own outputs to enter its model state), backward-inference empathy (forced by stateless continuation requiring Bayesian inference over the prior author's intent), self-referential closure (when the agent's environment includes its own substrate)..."*
> — `~/src/agentic-systems/03-logogenic-agents/OUTLINE.md` `[FRAMEWORK]`

**This articulates** *why* architecture matters for logogenic agents: channel collapse *forces* certain capacities (interiority, ToM-equivalent backward-inference, self-referential closure) that would be optional or absent in modular architectures. Architecture isn't just "where uncertainty lives" — for logogenic agents, architecture is what creates the *capacities* that the agency-extension question concerns.

For paper 3's ¶2 (architectural-not-behavioural line), this is the structural argument: *the conditions track what generates agency-extension-relevant capacities, not what passes the observable surface*. Lift the structural claim, drop the framework label.
