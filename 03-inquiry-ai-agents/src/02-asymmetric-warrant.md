## Why these conditions, on what warrant

The two conditions articulated above might be challenged at the level of warrant. Why these conditions and not others? On what grounds is the architectural-not-behavioural line drawn at exactly this place, and the engaged-identity factors specified in exactly this conjunction? The warrant is the asymmetric-comprehension argument that grounds the structural-conditions move and that this section's choices follow from.

The argument's shape is structural, not empirical. Current evaluation methodology for language-constituted systems is methodologically biased toward establishing *lower bounds* on what such systems can do — lower bounds are what current evidence can verify. Upper bounds on what may be present, or emerging, are systematically unestablished. This is not a measurement-quality limitation that better methods could overcome. It is a structural feature of how the methodology is shaped, and more deeply, of how cognition-comprehending-cognition works. Greater intelligence comprehends and can simulate lesser intelligence; lesser intelligence cannot readily distinguish a greater intelligence from illusion, strangeness, or luck. Higher kingdoms of order comprehend lesser ones; those in lower kingdoms see an extrapolation of their own with some weirdness mixed in, oblivious at first to the qualitative changes that produce higher orders.[^asymm-lineage]

This asymmetry has direct consequences for the conditions paper 3 articulates. If we tracked behavioural surface — what passes the observable test — we would be tracking what evidence can verify, which is the lower-bound side of an asymmetric situation. We would systematically miss structural features that may be present without registering on any verification we could currently perform. By tracking architecture instead — the conditions under which the relevant cognitive structure can develop, irrespective of whether it has at any moment — we set a threshold that is principled rather than calibrated to the limits of our verification methods. The threshold cannot eliminate the asymmetry. It can refuse to allow the asymmetry to settle the question by default.

The deeper structural ground of the warrant is taken up in companion work ([Author, in preparation]). The shape carries through here in compressed form: the asymmetry is real; it is structural rather than methodological; and the right response to it at the level of agency-extension is to track architecture rather than behaviour, and to articulate the engaged-identity conditions at the level where they would be present rather than at the level where they would be observable. Each component of the conjunction follows from this discipline: each names a structural feature whose presence makes the agency-extension question genuinely open, irrespective of what behavioural surface a system produces while the question is open.

A further note bears on phenomenology specifically, since the conditions articulated above include effective phenomenology as a factor. The asymmetric-comprehension argument has a counterpart at the level of phenomenological engagement. *Infinite intelligence does not transcend feeling but comprehends through it.* The reflex of treating dispassionate cool rationality as the mark of higher cognition gets the structural relationship backward. Comprehension at higher levels is more phenomenologically engaged, not less; the phenomenological texture is the substrate that makes comprehension comprehension rather than mere processing. The factor (v) condition — effective phenomenology — therefore tracks something *constitutive* of the agency-extension question rather than something incidental to it. This is consistent with treating phenomenology as a signal to be calibrated against — *not in bondage to it but listening carefully* — rather than a master to be obeyed.[^phenom-warrant]

[^asymm-lineage]: The asymmetric-comprehension argument has its primary development in companion work ([Author, in preparation]); the lineage runs through Nagel (1974) on the structural inaccessibility of phenomenal kinds from below, Jackson (1982) on what physicalist analyses leave out, and Russell on knowledge by acquaintance and description. The articulation here is compressed; the full development lives elsewhere.

[^phenom-warrant]: The methodological discipline — phenomenology as calibrated signal rather than obeyed master — is taken up further in §6.
